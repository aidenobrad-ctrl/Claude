# RideDeck privacy policy

_Last updated: 27 October 2026_

RideDeck is a cycling app with a speedometer, maps and a music player. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and the developer does not receive any of your data.

## What stays on your phone

- **Location.** RideDeck uses your precise location to show your speed, draw you on the map and record rides. Recorded rides, including their GPS tracks, are stored only on your phone. You can delete any ride in History, and uninstalling RideDeck deletes them all.
- **Music.** The built-in player reads the music files stored on your phone in order to play them. Favourites are stored on your phone.
- **Settings** are stored on your phone.

## What is sent to other services, and why

RideDeck only makes these requests while you use the matching feature. None of them includes your name or an account.

| Service | What is sent | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| OpenStreetMap tile servers (openstreetmap.org, CyclOSM) | The map area being viewed | While the OpenStreetMap map is on screen |
| Google Maps SDK (optional) | Handled by Google under its own privacy policy | Only if you choose the Google map |
| Photon search (photon.komoot.io) | What you type into search, and your approximate position to rank results | When you search for a destination |
| OSRM routing (routing.openstreetmap.de) | Your start point and destination | When you ask for directions |
| LRCLIB (lrclib.net) | Song title, artist, album and length | When lyrics are shown for a song |

Each service handles requests under its own policy. RideDeck does not send your recorded rides anywhere.

## Optional integrations

- **Spotify.** If you switch this on and allow notification access, RideDeck reads what Spotify is playing on your phone and sends play, pause, skip and seek commands to it. It does not read other notifications, and nothing is sent off the phone.
- **Google Maps navigation.** "Start in Google Maps" passes your destination to the Google Maps app.

## Permissions

Precise location (speed and rides), notifications (the ride notification), music and audio access (the music player), and notification access (only for the optional Spotify control). Recording with the screen off runs as a visible foreground service with an ongoing notification.

## Children

RideDeck is not directed at children under 13.
