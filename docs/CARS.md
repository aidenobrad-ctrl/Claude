# Cars

Every number here is measured by the automated suite (`tests/physics.test.ts`) on flat dry asphalt with default aids. 0–100 and 0–200 use launch control and TCS, braking uses ABS, and the skidpad has a 40 m radius with ESC off.

Each car is checked against a real-world reference for a comparable car of its class, and must land within 10%. Road tires must stay below 1.3 g.

## Reference values

**Halden Aster GT:** front-engine RWD coupe, twin-turbo I6, sport tires. Real cars of this size and power on summer tires run about 4.4 s to 100, 15 s to 200, 33 m from 100 to 0, about 1.0 g on the skidpad and 290 km/h flat out. Every measured value is within 10%.
