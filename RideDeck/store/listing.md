# Play Store listing text

**App name:** RideDeck

**Short description (80 characters max):**
Speedometer, cycling maps and music in one screen. No ads, no accounts.

**Full description:**

RideDeck puts everything you need on a ride on one screen: a big, clear speedometer, a cycling map with directions, and your music.

SPEEDOMETER AND RIDE TRACKING
• Huge, glanceable speed in km/h or mph
• Distance, moving time, average and max speed, odometer (hide any of them)
• Auto-pause when you stop, speed alert with sound and vibration
• Keeps recording with the screen off; every ride saved to History with a map of your route

MAPS AND DIRECTIONS
• Beautiful, smooth vector maps that rotate and tilt, with colour, clean and dark looks
• Search any destination and get cycling directions with turn-by-turn steps, ETA and spoken turns
• Automatic re-routing when you go off route

MUSIC
• Built-in player for the music on your phone, with synced lyrics
• Or control Spotify without leaving the app
• Seek bar, shuffle, favourites, lock-screen and headset controls

MADE FOR RIDING
• Pick any mix of map, speedometer and music, with a separate layout for landscape
• Drag the speedometer anywhere over the map
• Glove-friendly buttons, OLED black theme, Ride Mode that locks distracting screens while you move
• Scales to any screen, from compact phones to the Galaxy S Ultra and tablets

PRIVATE
No account, no ads, no analytics. Your rides stay on your phone.
